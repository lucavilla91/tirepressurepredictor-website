01

Run Filter

Pick exactly which training runs feed the prediction.

Scatter selectionRuns plotted by ambient temperature and best lap time. Drag a region or step ±0.5 °C / ±0.5 s to match race-day conditions.
Runs summaryPer-run detail, a laps histogram (green = enough laps, amber = fewer) and a warning when your target laps exceed available data.
Why it mattersExclude outlier sessions so the model learns from data that actually represents the stint you're predicting.
02

Temperature Corrections

Three decomposed corrections, per wheel — not one rule of thumb.

ΔT.AmbAmbient change between training and target conditions, applied through the ideal gas law.
ΔT.TrackTrack-surface temperature shift handled separately from air temperature.
T.Tires@PressCorrects when tires are pressurized at a different temperature than ambient. Each correction shows per-wheel deltas with a color-coded magnitude bar.
03

Bleed Correction

Know exactly how much to bleed on the grid.

Expansion ratioR = P_hot / P_cold computed per wheel from an exponential build-up model fitted to your telemetry.
Three modesConstant Bleed, Custom Start Pressure and Grid Pressure (enter measured pressures from a practice grid).
Ratio trendPer-wheel R over laps with a formation-lap marker — see precisely when and how much pressure builds.
04

Tire Pressure Logs

For long runs, endurance and cars without TPMS.

Compound trackingCold/hot pressures, compounds and air/track temperature per run, with ΔP vs ΔT charts and trend lines.
K-factor regressionAuto-fit per-wheel thermal expansion, plus a gas law calculator for hot/cold conversion.
Plateau auto-saveStabilized runs (97 % build-up) are detected from telemetry and saved as verified baselines automatically.
